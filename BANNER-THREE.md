# Banner thứ ba — Essentials

Tạo bằng Imagegen tích hợp theo yêu cầu bổ sung đủ 3 ảnh cho carousel. Dựa trên ảnh chiến dịch đầu và ảnh sản phẩm gile Essential xanh rêu, áo phao Classic đen. Đây là hình minh họa dựng cho website.

- PNG gốc: `assets/generated-banners/campaign-gile.png`.
- WebP website: `public/images/campaign-gile.webp`.
- Carousel: `src/app/hero-carousel.tsx`, dùng Swiper React với Navigation, Pagination, Keyboard và A11y.
- Không có nút Khám phá ngay trên cả 3 banner.

## Prompt cuối cùng

Use case: identity-preserve. Asset type: third THE Q fashion ecommerce carousel banner. Ultra-wide panorama, ratio 2.46:1, approximately 2464 x 1008 pixels. Image 1 is the identity and campaign-style reference: keep the same two adult Vietnamese models recognizable, the tranquil mountain lake, muted autumn forest, understated premium photography and soft overcast natural light. Image 2 is the precise garment reference for the man: dress him in this dark olive sleeveless horizontally quilted stand-collar zip-up gilet, worn over a simple ivory fine-knit long-sleeve sweater; match the vest shape, quilting and color. Image 3 is the garment reference for the woman: dress her in a feminine fit black stand-collar quilted zip-up puffer inspired by this jacket, with simple cream trousers. These are different outfits from the earlier olive hooded coat and cream puffer campaign. Pose both naturally, upper-thigh framing, both full heads visible with generous headroom. Keep the man around 65 percent image width, woman around 83 percent image width. Keep LEFT 47 percent as uncluttered pale lake and softly blurred neutral misty hills for later website title overlay. Sophisticated editorial fashion composition, realistic fabric and hands, subtle muted cool grey and olive palette, calm confident expressions. One cohesive photo. NO text or typography at all, no call-to-action, no slogan, no feature labels, no watermark, no borders or collage. Do not add text or buttons to the image.
